About

I'm Kallol — a builder working at the intersection of fitness and technology.

I'm the founder of Omni Coach, a platform that helps coaches and gym owners manage their athletes, track performance data, and run their business — all in one place.

Before Omni Coach I spent years working with endurance athletes and strength coaches, watching great coaching get buried under admin work. I started building to fix that.

I write here about product, engineering, fitness science, and whatever else is on my mind.
